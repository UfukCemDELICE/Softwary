# Split

A bill in cents is divided among people.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001-01:** The shares add up to the total. Covers EX-F1-01.
- **AC-001-02:** The cents that do not divide go one each to the first people. Covers EX-F1-01.
- **AC-001-03:** Fewer than one person is refused with status 2. Covers EX-F1-02.
